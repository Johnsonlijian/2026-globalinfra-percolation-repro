# External evaluation provenance

The JSON/CSV records here preserve the external-cohort roster, acquisition and
calibration records dated 5 September 2026. The roster timestamp precedes
acquisition, and the acquisition verification and calibration lock precede
external response calculation. These are author-held records, not a public
preregistration or independently timestamped registry.

The roster held out all 21 cities from the 71-city calibration set. It did
not certify that they were unseen in every earlier project analysis.
Stockholm and Cape Town were subsequently identified in manuscript history.
The final analysis therefore reports 19 primary cities, those two prior-use
cases separately, and all 21 acquired cities without error-based exclusion.
This provenance stratification is retrospective; original records were not
backdated or rewritten to describe it as prespecified.

registry_public.csv removes local raw-file paths from the intake registry.
No raw OSM graph or third-party network model is redistributed. Full hashes
refer to different source, serialized, or normalized graph objects as
specified in each record and are not interchangeable.
